About Me

I'm an engineering and technology student from Sri Lanka with a passion for robotics, embedded systems, and artificial intelligence. I build and experiment with hardware and software across multiple domains: robotics automation, computer vision, embedded systems programming, and electronics design.

My hands-on projects span Raspberry Pi-based systems, microcontroller programming, sensor integration, circuit design, and 3D printing. I'm particularly interested in how robotics, AI, and electronics converge to solve real-world problems.

Long-term, I'm pursuing robotics engineering, mechatronics, AI systems, and embedded systems development. Each project I undertake builds toward these goals by developing deeper technical expertise and problem-solving skills.
